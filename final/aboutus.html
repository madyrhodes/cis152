Who We Are

In 2020, when 1,119 acres of land were returned to the Esselen Tribe of Monterey County, this Native American Tribe wanted to preserve this precious natural landscape while continuining to share the cultures and traditions of the indigineous peoples of this area with those who value their honored history. The mission of the Esselen Tribal members who operate Pacific Trails Resort is to allow guests to live in close connection to the natural beauty of the Big Sur landscape as the tribe has for centuries but with the addition of modern comforts to ensure a pleasant and relaxing experience for all.

Our Mission

The team at Pacific Trails Resort are dedicated to sharing the history and culture of the Esselen People with guests through the design of the resort and the activities offered. Guests can expect to feel fully immersed in the same natural landscape which the tribe have been nurturing their own connection to for centuries. Here guests can expect to discover their own sense of connection to the land either through outdoor adventures such as kayaking or quiet meditation in the Big Sur wilderness.

Immerse Yourself

We look forward to welcoming you to our resort and sharing the beauty of native heritage in Big Sur with you. Pacific Trails Resort is the perfect destination for those who want to become deeply immersed in history, culture and nature while enjoying the comforts of a luxury resort.
